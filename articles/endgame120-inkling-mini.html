Adding Inkling-2-Mini to Endgame-120

Inkling 2 Mini is a new, small model by thinking machines. Based on published benchmarks it is the top model for its class. Let’s test it on our reasoning benchmark. Other models in the chart are much larger/more expensive, so it is expected to perform below them.

Its performance is very strong, but it’s consuming even more tokens than deepseek-flash and inkling-small.

There’s also more variance at the higher range of effort - potentially caused by quality drop at longer context, but that needs to be tested separately.
